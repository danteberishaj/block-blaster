Rowflare is an offline-first block puzzle for Android and iOS.

## Quick help

- **A shape will not move:** dim shapes do not fit the current board. Try
  another shape, Shuffle, Break, or Hint.
- **Prefer tapping:** tap a tray shape, then tap a highlighted board cell.
- **An ad did not grant a helper:** a helper is granted only after the ad reports
  completion. A skipped, closed, unavailable, or interrupted ad does not grant
  a reward. Try again later; no score or board progress is removed.
- **Restore a run:** return to the home screen and choose **Resume**. Starting a
  new run permanently replaces the current board.
- **No sound:** use the speaker control and check the device's media volume.
- **Reset local data:** use the operating system's app-storage controls or
  uninstall and reinstall the app. This removes the active run and local scores.

When reporting a problem, include the device model, OS version, Rowflare version,
what you expected, what happened, and whether it can be repeated. Do not send
passwords or payment information.
