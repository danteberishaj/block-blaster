## Summary

Rowflare is an offline-first puzzle game. It does not require an account. Game
progress and preferences are stored on the player's device. On Android, a
player may choose to view an optional rewarded ad after a helper reaches zero.

## Data stored by Rowflare

Rowflare stores the active run, scores, helper balances, rewarded-helper limits,
sound preference, and tutorial status in local application storage. This data
is used to restore play and preferences. The publisher does not receive it from
the app. It remains until the player clears app data or uninstalls the app,
subject to operating-system backup behavior.

## Optional rewarded ads

The Android app integrates Unity Ads. An ad starts only after the player chooses
**Watch Ad**. Rowflare requests non-personalized/contextual ads. Unity may still
process information such as the advertising identifier, IP address, device and
operating-system details, approximate location derived from network data, ad
interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals to deliver and measure
ads.

Unity's handling and retention of that information is governed by its own
privacy materials: <https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy>.

The iOS version does not currently integrate rewarded ads. The publisher must
update this policy before adding an iOS ad SDK or changing personalization.
